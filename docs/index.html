Hi, welcome to CopyVault local-first clipboard manager.

Engineered Without Compromise

Every feature in CopyVault is crafted for native speed, zero telemetry, and effortless desktop productivity.

⚡

Sub-15ms FTS5 Search

Powered by embedded SQLite with full-text search (FTS5) and BM25 relevance ranking. Find any clipping across tens of thousands of items instantaneously.

🛡️

Zero-Telemetry Privacy

Your clipboard never touches external servers. Automatically honors secret concealment flags from 1Password, Bitwarden, KeePassXC, and Keychain.

🖱️

Drag-and-Drop Pasting

Drag clippings directly from CopyVault cards straight into browser form fields, code editors, or desktop input fields.

🔁

Sequential Paste Queues

Select multiple clips in custom order (1, 2, 3...) and paste them sequentially or copy them joined by custom separators.

📑

Reusable Snippets

Store permanent templates with trigger prefixes (e.g., !email) and automatic placeholder expansion for dates, times, and clipboard text.

🖼️

Rich Media & Images

Capture images, screenshots, and web graphics with automatic local disk caching, fast thumbnails, and high-resolution lightbox previews.

🔒 Privacy & Security

Your Data Stays on Your Machine

We believe your clipboard is private. CopyVault was built from day one with a zero-network architecture.

✓

100% Offline by Design

CopyVault includes zero analytics, zero trackers, and no background network connections. No Google Analytics, no telemetry, ever.

✓

One-Click Complete Data Purge

You have full ownership of your data. Clear your history or delete individual records at any time directly inside the app.

✓

Local SQLite Storage

All items and cached thumbnails reside strictly in standard user-scoped desktop application folders.

Hi, I'm Sandeep

Independent Software Developer

I built CopyVault because existing clipboard managers were either bloated electron apps consuming hundreds of megabytes of RAM or closed-source utilities that risk syncing confidential clippings to cloud servers.

CopyVault is built natively with Tauri 2, Rust, and React 19 to provide a sub-10MB native footprint, blazing speed, and 100% open-source transparency.
